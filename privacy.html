Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Your privacy matters to us at Motapa Calculator. This policy explains what information is collected when you use our Website, how it is used, and what choices you have regarding your data. By using our Website, you agree to this policy.

1. What Information We Collect

Most features of Motapa Calculator do not require you to provide any personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number). The information you enter into our calculators — such as weight, height, age, gender, and activity level — is used only to perform calculations.

The following data is stored locally in your own browser (via localStorage), not sent to any server of ours:

  • Your food diary entries
  • Your weekly weight progress
  • Your saved weekly food plans
  • Your selected language and other preferences

This means this information exists only on your own device. If you clear your browser data, switch devices, or use a different browser, this saved information will no longer be available, since it is never stored on a central server.

2. Cookies

Our Website uses cookies to improve your experience, such as remembering your selected language. In addition, to keep the Website free, we use third-party advertising services, particularly Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show you ads that are relevant based on your visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our Website and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

3. Third-Party Advertising Services

We use Google AdSense and potentially other third-party advertising networks to cover the costs of keeping the Website free. These advertising networks use their own technology (such as cookies, web beacons, and JavaScript) to deliver ads, and in this process, they may automatically collect your IP address. These technologies are used to measure ad effectiveness and personalize advertising content.

We have no control over data collected by third-party advertising companies. We recommend reviewing Google's own privacy policy to fully understand how they use your data.

4. Analytics Tools

We may use analytics tools to understand and improve our Website's performance, which collect information such as how many people visit the site, which pages are most popular, and what device or browser visitors use. This information is collected in aggregate and does not reveal the identity of individual users.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it. As noted elsewhere in this policy, most user data never leaves the child's own device in the first place, which further limits this risk.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. Since most of your personal information (food diary, weight progress, saved plans) is stored locally on your own device and never transmitted to a central server, the risk of data leakage is significantly reduced. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Our Website uses HTTPS encryption to keep data transmission between your browser and our server secure, and we periodically review our practices to align with current security standards.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Since most of your data is stored on your own device, you have full control over it:

  • You can delete your food diary, weight progress, or saved plans at any time using the "Clear" buttons built into the Website.
  • You can clear all cookies and locally stored data through your browser's settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising using the methods described above.
  • You can switch the site's language at any time, which does not affect any of your previously stored data.
  • If the laws of your region (such as the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA) grant you additional rights, we make every effort to comply with those laws.

8. International Users

Motapa Calculator is available in over 100 languages for users around the world. If you use our Website from outside Pakistan, including from the European Union or the United States, please be aware that your data (particularly through third-party advertising cookies) may be transferred across international borders to servers operated by our advertising and analytics partners. By continuing to use our Website, you consent to this.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new services, legal requirements, or improved practices. Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

10. Links to Other Websites

Our Website may occasionally include links to external websites, such as reference articles or resources. This Privacy Policy applies only to Motapa Calculator. When you click an external link, you become subject to that website's own privacy policy, which is outside our control. We recommend reviewing any external website's privacy policy before providing personal information there.

12. How Long We Keep Data

Because most of your data (food diary, weight progress, saved plans) lives only in your browser's local storage, it remains there until you delete it yourself, clear your browser data, or switch devices — we have no ability to retain, back up, or restore this information on our end. For our saved plans feature specifically, entries older than 30 days are automatically removed to keep your storage tidy; this happens entirely on your device and does not involve any of our servers.

Any data that third-party advertising or analytics services collect (such as cookies) is retained according to those third parties' own retention policies, which are outside our control. Please refer to Google's privacy documentation for details on how long advertising cookies are retained.

13. Language and Translation

Motapa Calculator is available in over 100 languages. This Privacy Policy is provided primarily in English and Urdu; other language versions of the site link back to this policy. If any translated summary of our privacy practices conflicts with this English or Urdu version, this version shall govern. We are continually working to expand full translations of our policy pages to more languages over time.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy (for example, wanting information about data stored about you), please contact us through our Contact page.

14. Your Consent

By using Motapa Calculator, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes to how we collect or use data, we will update this page and, where appropriate, provide a more prominent notice. Your continued use of the Website after any such update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, we recommend discontinuing use of the Website and clearing any locally stored data through your browser settings.
