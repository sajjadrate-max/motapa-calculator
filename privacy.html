Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters, and this website has been designed with that principle in mind from the start. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use this calculator, and how it is handled.

1. No Personal Data Collection. This website operates entirely within your own browser. The weight, height, age, gender, activity level, and food selections you enter are used only to perform calculations locally on your device. This information is never transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any external server.

2. No Accounts or Sign-ups. There is no requirement to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personally identifiable information in order to use this calculator.

3. Third-Party Advertising. This website may, now or in the future, display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense. These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data in this context, and manage your ad preferences, through Google's own Ads Settings and Privacy policies.

4. Cookies. Aside from any cookies placed by third-party advertising or analytics providers described above, this website does not set its own tracking cookies to identify or follow individual users across sessions. Your language preference and cookie-consent choice are saved locally on your own device only.

5. Children's Privacy. This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is intended for general audience use, with guidance from a parent or guardian recommended for minors.

6. Changes to This Policy. This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the Contact page of this website.

8. Data Retention. Food diary data automatically clears at the start of each new day, while your weight progress log is kept until you choose to delete it via the "Clear All Entries" button. All of this data lives only in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to any server.

9. Your Rights. Since no data is collected on any server, you have full control to permanently delete your own data (diary, weight log, selected foods) at any time, either by clearing your browser settings or via the "clear" buttons built into the site.

10. International Users. This website serves users worldwide. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, concerns around cross-border data transfer do not apply.
