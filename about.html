About Us

Welcome to Motapa Calculator, your premier online destination for personalized health, weight management, and nutritional planning. Our platform is meticulously designed to help individuals decode their body metrics and achieve sustainable wellness goals.

We combine established scientific equations such as the Mifflin-St Jeor formula for BMR calculation with practical dietary frameworks tailored for various lifestyles, including traditional South Asian preferences and daily fitness tracking.

Our mission is to provide free, accessible, and accurate calorie, BMI, and TDEE calculation tools to empower users worldwide in multiple languages.
