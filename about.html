About Us

Motapa Calculator is a free online platform built to help people understand their body weight, health, and daily nutritional needs in a simple, accessible way. Our goal is to take complex scientific calculations — like BMI, BMR, and TDEE — and make them so easy that anyone can understand their current situation in a few seconds and build a practical plan around it.

Obesity and weight-related health issues (such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease) are rising quickly across South Asia and many other regions. A big part of the problem is that most health calculators and information sites are only available in English, leaving millions of people unable to benefit from them. We built Motapa Calculator to close that gap — first in Urdu, and later in over 100 other languages, so that as many people as possible could use it.

What We Offer

Our website gives users a complete, entirely free toolkit that includes:

  • BMI Calculator: Calculates your Body Mass Index and tells you whether your weight falls in the normal, underweight, overweight, or obese range.
  • BMR & TDEE Calculator: Shows how many calories your body burns at rest, and your total daily energy expenditure once daily activity is factored in.
  • Personalized diet plan: Daily calorie, protein, fat, and carbohydrate targets based on your age, height, weight, and activity level.
  • Automatic and manual food selection: Choose your own foods, or let the system automatically suggest a balanced plan — including local and desi dishes like biryani and daal chawal.
  • Food diary: Log what you eat each day and automatically track your remaining calorie budget.
  • Weekly weight progress tracker: Log your weight over time and see your progress as a graph.
  • Saved plans: Save your selected foods with the date and review them anytime later.
  • Daily walking plan: A recommended daily walking target to support your weight loss goals.
  • Free educational articles: Dozens of detailed, reliable articles on BMI, calories, exercise, and healthy eating, in both Urdu and English.

Our Philosophy

We believe that basic health information and tools should be a right for everyone, not something locked behind an expensive app or consultant. That's why Motapa Calculator is completely free, with no registration, subscription, or payment required. Anyone, anywhere, can open it on their phone or computer and get a picture of their health in seconds.

We also understand that everyone's life, culture, and eating habits are different. That's why our diet plans include local and desi foods, so users get advice that actually fits their day-to-day life rather than generic, hard-to-follow rules. Our goal is practical, achievable, and sustainable advice — not rigid diet regimens that are impossible to stick to.

We also recognize that language is often the biggest barrier to accessing health information. That's why our website is available not just in English and Urdu, but in over 100 languages worldwide — including Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, French, Spanish, and many African and Asian languages. Our goal is for anyone, anywhere in the world, to be able to understand their own health in their own language, without needing to rely on a translator or a second language they're less comfortable with.

Our Technology and Standards

All calculations on Motapa Calculator are based on well-established, medically recognized formulas (such as the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for BMR, and World Health Organization BMI standards). We regularly update and refine our calculators to give users the most accurate and reliable results possible.

Our website also functions as a Progressive Web App (PWA), meaning it can be installed like an app on mobile devices and works quickly even on limited internet connections. Your data — such as your food diary, weight progress, and saved plans — is stored directly on your own device, keeping things both private and fast.

Who This Is For

Motapa Calculator is designed for people of every age and background. Whether you want to lose weight, gain weight, or simply understand your current health status, this tool is built for you.

  • People trying to lose weight: Get a clear daily calorie target and a practical diet plan suited to your body and lifestyle.
  • People trying to gain weight: Underweight users also get a plan for gaining weight in a healthy way.
  • Athletes and fitness enthusiasts: Track daily protein, carbohydrate, and calorie needs accurately.
  • Families building healthier habits: Parents can monitor their own and their children's weight.
  • Beginners: Our educational articles are a great starting point for anyone wanting to learn the basics of calories, metabolism, and healthy eating.

We've intentionally kept the website simple and straightforward to use. No complicated setup, no account required — just enter your weight, height, age, and activity level, and get instant results. Whether you're checking your BMI for the first time or you've been tracking your weight loss journey for months using our progress tracker, the experience is designed to stay fast, clear, and free of clutter.

We also know that motivation can be hard to sustain over weeks and months. That's why features like the weekly progress graph and saved food plans exist — to give you a visual, tangible sense of how far you've come, rather than leaving you to guess. Small, consistent steps, tracked over time, tend to produce far better long-term results than short bursts of extreme effort.

Why We Built This

The idea for Motapa Calculator came from a simple observation: millions of people in Pakistan and across South Asia want to understand their weight and health better, but most of the tools available online are either too technical, too expensive, or simply not in a language they're comfortable reading. We wanted to change that by building something genuinely useful, genuinely free, and genuinely accessible — regardless of a person's income, education level, or native language.

Since launch, we've continued to expand the platform based on what real users have asked for — from a food diary and weight progress tracker to saved diet plans and a growing library of educational articles. Every feature we add is guided by one question: does this make it easier for an ordinary person to understand and manage their own health?

An Important Note

Motapa Calculator is an educational and informational tool. It is not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian, or medical professional. Our calculators provide estimates based on general scientific formulas, while every person's health situation is unique. If you have a serious medical condition, chronic illness, are pregnant, or have any special physical circumstances, please consult your doctor or a registered dietitian before making any major changes to your diet or exercise routine. See our Disclaimer page for more details.
