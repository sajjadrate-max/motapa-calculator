About Us

Welcome to this weight loss and nutrition calculator. It was built for one simple reason: people trying to lose weight often struggle not because they lack willpower, but because they lack clear, personalized numbers to work with. Generic advice like "eat less and move more" rarely helps anyone succeed on its own.

This calculator uses your weight, height, age, gender, and daily activity level to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) using the well-established Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is widely used in clinical nutrition. From there, it calculates a safe daily calorie target for weight loss, along with recommended protein, fat, and carbohydrate amounts in grams, so you know exactly what a balanced day of eating should look like for your specific body.

Beyond the numbers, this site includes a diet planning tool that lets you select real foods — chicken, rice, roti, daal, vegetables, fruits, dairy, oils, and nuts — and see in real time how they add up against your daily targets. You can build your own meal plan manually, food by food, checking totals as you go.

Alternatively, the website can automatically suggest a complete meal plan for you, adjusted according to the current season, since eating patterns often change between summer and winter — favoring cooling foods like yogurt, cucumber, and watermelon in hot weather, and warming foods like nuts, oil, and heartier proteins in cold weather.

This calculator also recommends a daily walking distance, based on your body weight, to help you close the gap between the calories you eat and the calories you burn — because sustainable weight loss usually works best when diet and activity are addressed together, not separately.

The goal here is not to sell a quick fix or a fad diet. It is to give you a clear, evidence-based starting point that you can adjust over time, ideally alongside guidance from a qualified doctor or nutritionist. This website is provided free of charge and works entirely in your browser — no account, no sign-up, and no data sent anywhere.

A Food Diary ("Log As You Eat") is also part of this site, letting you record what you eat throughout the day — breakfast, lunch, or any snack — with your remaining daily calories, protein, fat, and carbohydrates updating automatically. Once a particular nutrient quota is used up, foods from that category are automatically removed from the list so you don't exceed your daily target.

A Weekly Progress section lets you log your weight regularly and see your trend on a visual graph over time, alongside a comparison of how your actual weekly weight loss compares to the calculator's projected estimate. This data stays entirely on your own device.

This website is available in more than 85 languages, including Urdu, Hindi, Arabic (with Gulf-region dialect variants), English, French, Spanish, Chinese, and many more, so that people around the world can plan their nutrition comfortably in their own language. Your language preference is saved in your browser so you don't need to select it again each visit.

Our goal is transparency: the formulas used are publicly published and well-established in clinical nutrition — there is no hidden algorithm or agenda. We want you to understand where your numbers come from, not just take them on faith.
